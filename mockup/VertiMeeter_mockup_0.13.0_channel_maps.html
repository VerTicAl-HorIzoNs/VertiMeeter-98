Columns are the buses you have. Ticked buses are summed into that channel. A stereo row sends the bus left on the first channel, right on the second. A mono row sends L, R or L+R (the average) of the ticked buses. An empty row sends silence. Summing can go over full scale: the sender status then says "summed channels clipped N samples" (nothing is limited for you).
